NeuroInsight

🧠 About NeuroInsight

NeuroInsight is an AI-powered web application designed to assist in the early detection and classification of brain tumors using MRI scans. Leveraging advanced deep learning models, NeuroInsight can accurately classify scans into four categories: Glioma Tumor, Meningioma Tumor, Pituitary Tumor, and No Tumor.

This platform offers a seamless and intuitive interface where users can upload MRI images and receive real-time predictions backed by cutting-edge Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) architectures. Beyond diagnosis, NeuroInsight ensures that users can store and download their classification results, enabling better tracking and medical record management.

💡 Key Features:

  • • Upload MRI scans for instant brain tumor classification
  • • Interactive prediction history with patient name, scan, and tumor type
  • • Clickable tumor categories with detailed medical insights
  • • Option to download results as a report for further reference
  • • Secure storage of prediction history for review and analysis
  • • Clean, responsive user interface and reliable backend integration

🎯 Our Vision:

To empower clinical diagnostics with AI precision—making brain tumor screening faster, smarter, and more accessible to healthcare practitioners and individuals alike.
